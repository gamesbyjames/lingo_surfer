Many hands. One little journey.

Runner and motion

The Xbot character and its skeletal idle, walk, and run animations are from Adobe Mixamo, as used and credited in the official Three.js r170 animation example. Jump and slide poses are added by this game. Adobe/Mixamo rights and usage terms apply to these assets.

Rendering

Three.js, released under the MIT license. The coastal scenery, procedural textures, water, particles, and interface were created for Lingo Surfer.

Typography and speech

DM Sans and Manrope via Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).

Greek pronunciation is recorded with ElevenLabs Eleven v3 using Stavros — Native Greek, Warm & playful, a standard-accent Greek voice from the ElevenLabs Voice Library. The lesson recordings are generated in advance and served with the game; browser speech synthesis is not used.

Hindi pronunciation uses Sanchit K — Real, Warm Healthcare Agent, a standard-accent Hindi voice from the ElevenLabs Voice Library, with original Devanagari text and separate masculine/feminine sentence recordings.
